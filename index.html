How Ten-God Career Diagnosis Works

A structured translation of your Bazi energy into modern career language — a map, not a verdict.

1. Build the Four Pillars

From your birth date we derive the Year, Month, Day and (if known) Hour pillars — the foundation of Bazi.

2. Read the Ten Gods

Each pillar is interpreted as a "Ten God" relative to your Day Master, revealing how you work, create, lead and earn.

We cross your energy profile with favorable elements to suggest industries, side-business tracks and timing.
